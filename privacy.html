Privacy policy

This Google OAuth client is used only by its owner, with the open-source tool rclone, to access the owner's own Google Drive accounts.

All data moves directly between Google's servers and the owner's own computer. No data is collected, stored, shared, sold or sent to any other party or server by this client.

Access tokens are stored only on the owner's computer, and access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Questions can be raised by opening an issue in this repository.
